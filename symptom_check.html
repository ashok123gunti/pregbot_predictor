Self-Care Tips
Rest

Get adequate sleep and take breaks during the day.

Hydration

Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily to reduce swelling.

Small Meals

Eat small, frequent meals to manage nausea.

Gentle Movement

Light walking can help with back pain and digestion.
